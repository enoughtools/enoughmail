# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention and publication of private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the EnoughTools GitHub organization; do not publish another person's private information in a report. Maintainers should handle reports fairly and discreetly.
